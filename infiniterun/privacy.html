Privacy policy

Minesweeper Infinite Run · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Minesweeper Infinite Run ("the game") is made by Naassom Dev Studio. You can reach us at naassomdevstudio@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, gold, diamonds, cards, worlds, world pictures, settings and your miner's look) is saved on your device only. We don't run an account system and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We cannot see your saves.

You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete my data. Deleting the app does the same.

Services the game uses

  • Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores to the leaderboards. Apple shows them with your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy applies. To remove your scores, use the Game Center settings on your iPhone, or contact Apple.
  • Google AdMob (ads). The game shows video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads performed, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the game asks your permission before ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites; if you say no, you still see ads, but not personalized ones. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat). When you buy gold, diamonds or unlimited lives, Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, which helps us check purchases, receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your purchase history for this app. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your saves in Settings, as above.
  • Turn off ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Ask questions or requests at naassomdevstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
