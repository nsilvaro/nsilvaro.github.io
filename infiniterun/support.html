Support

Minesweeper Infinite Run

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to naassomdevstudio@gmail.com. In the game, Settings → Report a bug opens an email with your device details filled in.

My purchase is missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases. It brings back the ad-free bundle and Stress-free play. Gold, diamonds and unlimited-lives packs are used up when you spend them and can't be restored. If you were charged and didn't get them, email us with your Apple receipt.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device only. We don't have accounts, so we can't move it for you. Restoring an iPhone backup onto the new phone may bring it back, but we can't promise that.

Delete my data

Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete my data erases everything the game saved on your device. Your Game Center scores are managed in your iPhone's Game Center settings.

I'm out of lives

Lives come back over time. You can also watch a short video for one, or buy Stress-free play so lives never run out.
