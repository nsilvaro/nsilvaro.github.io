NumberPaint Connect

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to naassomdevstudio@gmail.com.

How do I play?

Every number has a partner in the same color. Draw a path between them that is exactly as long as the number, counting both ends: a 4 takes four cells. Paths can't cross, and every cell is used. A 1 is finished on its own. Fill the board to reveal the picture. Settings → How to play shows the rules again.

My purchase is missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases. It brings back Remove Ads and Unlimited Photo Puzzles on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. If you were charged and still don't have them, email us with your Apple receipt.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device only. We don't have accounts, so we can't move it for you. Restoring an iPhone backup onto the new phone may bring it back, but we can't promise that. Purchases always come back with Restore purchases.

How many photo puzzles can I keep?

The first 5 are free. With Unlimited Photo Puzzles you can make as many as your iPhone has room for (up to 200 kept at a time). They're stored on your iPhone only: they're included in your iPhone's backups, and deleting the app deletes them. You can delete one on its own in My Photos (the ⋯ on its card); a free one you used stays used.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. A photo puzzle is made on your phone from the photo you pick, and stays there.

Delete my data

Settings → Delete my data erases all your progress on this phone. Purchases stay with your Apple ID.
