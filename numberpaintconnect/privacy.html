Privacy policy

NumberPaint Connect · Last updated: November 10, 2026

NumberPaint Connect ("the game") is made by Naassom Dev Studio. You can reach us at naassomdevstudio@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, times, puzzles in progress, your photo puzzles and your settings) is saved on your device only. We don't run accounts and never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. We cannot see your saves. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings → Delete my data. Deleting the app does the same.

Your photos

When you make a photo puzzle, the game reads only the photo you pick, turns it into a puzzle on your device, and keeps the puzzle there. Your photos are never uploaded or shared. Saving a finished picture to Photos happens only when you tap Save.

Services the game uses

  • Google AdMob (ads). The game shows ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance information, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the game asks your permission before ads may track you across other companies' apps and websites; if you say no, you still see ads, but not personalized ones. In the EU and UK, Google's consent form asks first. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat). When you buy Remove Ads or Unlimited Photo Puzzles, Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, which helps us check purchases, receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

The game has no analytics and no servers of its own.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your progress in Settings, as above.
  • Turn off ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Questions or requests: naassomdevstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
